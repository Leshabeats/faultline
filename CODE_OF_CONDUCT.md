# Code of Conduct

Faultline is an open project for people learning, practicing, and teaching system design. Be curious, specific, and generous with context.

We do not tolerate harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, or deliberately disruptive behavior. Critique ideas and implementations, not people. Respect different experience levels and avoid presenting one architecture as universally correct without stating its assumptions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
